Runner

Jet plays the offline dinosaur game. The page describes what is ahead in a sentence (the obstacle, and whether it is about to hit), and repeatedly asks Jet two questions: a noul, "is an obstacle about to hit?", and a choice, "how does the dinosaur get past it?" (jump, duck or keep running). When Jet says yes, the dinosaur does the move Jet picked. Moves are sent to Jet on your browser’s GPU or a Jet server.

Space or ↑ to jump, ↓ to duck. Tap the game to jump.
